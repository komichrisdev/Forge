# Verification and precedence

`verification_status` records review state; `confidence` records bounded
certainty. Neither derives the other.

- `unverified`: source-grounded but not explicitly reviewed.
- `verified`: explicitly checked against the cited source manifests.
- `conflicted`: authoritative claims disagree; `## Conflicts` preserves them.
- `superseded`: retained history replaced by another canonical page.

Original authoritative sources beat generated summaries. Approved decisions
are not overturned by recency alone. Supporting evidence may strengthen a
claim but cannot silently replace a decision. Verification never hides dissent.
